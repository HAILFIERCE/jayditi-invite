# Jayditi Invite

A responsive, single-page wedding invitation for Jay & Aditi.

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies are required.

## Important details to personalise

- The date and time powering the countdown live at the top of `script.js`.
- Event dates, times, venue name, and map URL are in `index.html`.
- RSVP currently confirms on-screen only; connect the form submit handler in `script.js` to your preferred form service before inviting guests.
